## Radians are arc length

On the unit circle the arc swept by an angle $\theta$ has length $s = r\theta = \theta$. Drag the slider (or press play) to unroll the arc onto the line below: the highlighted segment on the line always has the same length as the highlighted arc.

Full turn: $\theta = 2\pi \approx 6.283$, so the circumference of the unit circle is $2\pi$.
