*Statistical Genomics* (UGent, MSc Bioinformatics / MSc Statistical Data Analysis, 2026 edition). Preprocessing, quantification and differential analysis of high-throughput omics data, with shotgun proteomics and bulk / single-cell RNA-seq as the running examples, plus experimental design. Tooling is R / Bioconductor.

The official material is **not copied here**: this page links to it. The notes in this section are my own, written topic by topic rather than lecture by lecture.

- Course site: [statomics.github.io/SGA](https://statomics.github.io/SGA/)
- Source repository: [statOmics/SGA](https://github.com/statOmics/SGA)
- Material © Lieven Clement, Ghent University, [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
